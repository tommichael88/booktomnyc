## 3. Architecture, as it is today (not as the target)

- **The page.** `qr.html` is one file. - **Two catalogs while the dual-source transition is open.** `ssot/btnyc_source.json` is the **entity-first authored source** and is authoritative: entities own their questions, uncertainty classes and services; services are entity + action + modifiers; ids everywhere; confidence, retirement, recovery and the three complexity axes are authored there. `btnyc.json` is what the runtime and the compiler still read, a **mirror** held to the source by `project_legacy.js --check`. After a source change: `migrate_legacy.js --write` only if its inputs changed (it refuses to overwrite a hand-edited source), then `publish.js`, then `report.js --write`; their `--check` modes are in the suite.

Today (**P1**) the source is hand-edited JSON, `btnyc.json` the hybrid mirror the runtime reads, and `ssot/derived/btnyc_graph.json` a published artifact with one runtime consumer. At completion (**P2**) `btnyc.json` is written only by the publish step (retargeted, not done) and the legacy authoring tools retire in one commit.
