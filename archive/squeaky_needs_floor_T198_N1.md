# T198 N1 — "squeaky" alone does not name a floor job

**Defect (real page).** "Fix squeaky door" (also "squeaky stairs", "squeaky bed") was quoted as *Squeaky Floor Repair* ($50/60 min): the objects entry for that service carried the bare keyword "squeaky". The golden-master corpus had recorded the wrong answer ("fix a squeaky door" → squeaky_floor_repair).
**Change (dual-source, by the documented path).** `ent.floor` aliases and the `intent_mappings.objects` entry: keyword "squeaky floor"; synonyms "squeaky floor(s)", "floor is/are squeaky", "floor squeaky", "floor squeak", "squeaky floor repair"; `full_weight_synonyms` for the non-keyword floor phrases (a plain synonym scores at half weight and would not carry the route). Source edited by hand, `ssot_patch.js` → `carry_charter.js --write` → `splice_compiled.py` → `project_legacy.js --check` and `validate_source.js` pass; derived graph, reports, provenance ledger and last-migration mark regenerated by their tools.
**After.** "Fix squeaky door" asks the door questions ("What's the issue with the door?"); "squeaky stairs"/"squeaky bed" go to the general intake; every floor phrasing that worked still reaches Squeaky Floor Repair ("my floor is squeaky", "the floors are squeaky", "squeaky floors", "squeaky floor in bedroom"), and "floor squeaks when I walk" now does too (it did not before).
