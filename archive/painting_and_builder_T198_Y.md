# T198 Z — mixed painting requests; the customer's action is kept (2026-11-25)

## Z1. Y1 counterexample (checked first, found, corrected)
Y1's `unless_any` (patch, hang, mount, drywall...) *suppressed* painting recognition. "paint the room and hang a picture" was priced as Shelf Mounting ($60) with no word about the painting: the customer's intent was discarded under a price that looked like it covered it; "repaint the bedroom and mount a tv", "install a shelf and paint the room" the same.
Shared correction (authored `global_rules.unsupported_trades[painting]`, one interpretation, no phrase list per case): the trade has two modes. `answer`: nothing we do is also asked for -> said, nothing priced (as Y1). `notice`: the request also names work we do (`alongside_any`: patch, hole, crack, drywall, hang, mount, install...) -> that work is quoted AND the page says painting isn't included. A service that authors the trade's own material (`covered_by_material` MAT-MSC-PaintTouchUp: Wall Hole or Crack Repair, "Painting included") covers it, so "patch and paint the wall" is that service with no disclaimer. Art senses ("a painting", artwork, canvas) and "myself" stay exceptions: "hang a painting" is a hanging job. Generic words (fix, repair, peeling, damaged, stain) are not "alongside": "paint is peeling on the wall" and "touch up paint" are answered as painting, not priced as Minor Home Repairs.
Known limit (recorded): "paint the walls and patch the crack" is the patch service, whose catalog text says painting is included (a touch-up of the patched spot); the whole-wall paint is not separately disclaimed because the authored service claims it.

## Z2. Requested action is its own fact
Trace: the customer's words -> `understandRequest` returned `intent.stype` = the resolved object's type (a blind's service is an Install) -> the builder seeded `BLD.action` from that -> "my blind is broken" became "I need to install my blind" (free text kept "fix": its ad-lib reads the typed verb). First divergence: the requested action and the service's type were one field.
Correction (owners: `modules/nlp_engine.js`, orchestrator context): `_detectStatedStype` (the customer's verb -> its authored service type, `action_conjugations`; an implied "fix" from a malfunction shares `detectActionsInOrder`'s rule) is returned as `understandRequest().statedStype`, carried on the booking context (`statedStype`) and the route (`requestedAction`), and the builder seeds from it. Install/hang/mount are one authored family, so the object's own Mount refinement still applies ("hang my clock" stays `mount`); a different kind of work (fix, assemble, set up) is never overridden by the object's default. Service identity and price are untouched (same authored service; the booking notes already carry the customer's own words, `S.desc`).
Measured: of 4,455 generated phrases the stated type differs from the object's default in ~2,000 builder seeds (the verb the customer said now wins; e.g. "fix lock"). Walked on the page, `main` vs branch, for the incomplete requests that open the builder (fix lock/disposal/overhead fan/ice maker/bulb/trim): the final totals are identical ($70, $65, $45, $65, $45, $70); the session now carries `fix` instead of `install` and asks what is wrong (the repair question `main` skipped).
