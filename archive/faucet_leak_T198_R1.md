# T198 R1 — a leak at the faucet is the faucet repair

**Defect.** "Faucet leak", "bathroom faucet leak" and "kitchen faucet leaking" were quoted as *Under-Sink Leak Repair* ($75): the faucet object won, and the description-sibling scoring picked the leak service on the leftover word "leak". "Water leak from the faucet", "water leak at the faucet", "tap is leaking" got no service: the generic `leak` object took them at full weight. ("Faucet leaking", "faucet is leaking", "dripping faucet" already worked.)
**Change (data only).** Two contextual overrides, by the dual-source path: on the `faucet` object, a leak word (leak/leaks/leaking/leaky) with faucet/tap → `faucet_repair_drip` ($50), placed after the under-sink and drain overrides so those still win; on the generic `leak` object, a faucet/tap word → the same service, after the under-the-sink override. The O1 under-the-sink overrides also learn the hyphenated "under-sink" so the service's own name ("Under-sink leak repair on dripping faucet", a chip-builder sentence) stays the leak job.
**After.** All faucet-leak phrasings → Faucet Drip Repair. Unchanged: leaks under/at the sink, a faucet leaking under the sink, kitchen sink leaking, washing-machine leaks at the tap, clogged sink, install a sink.
**Measured.** Golden master 2,988 points vs Q1: 0 differ. Typed corpora vs Q1: counts 275 unchanged; definitions 357 → 2 changed, wide 7,803 → 49 changed, all "faucet a pipe leak [on …]" (the builder's object+leak sentence: no service → Faucet Drip Repair, $50 base with that modifier's own fee). The one regression the first pass produced (the Under-Sink Leak Repair name itself) was fixed with the hyphen keywords and re-measured.
**Known, unchanged.** "Pipe leak", "water leak" and "hose bib leaking" get the general intake; an outdoor faucet leak is a Faucet Drip Repair (as before).
